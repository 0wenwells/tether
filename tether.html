Cards in view grow tubes. Choose how they connect.

  1. Use 1 to 4 cards, printed at 100% (50 mm black squares) or shown on phone screens.
  2. One card grows a tube with a free end. Each extra card can catch an end.
  3. Pick a Mode: Chain threads one cable through every card in number order. Pairs joins 0–1 and 2–3. Hub runs a tube from the lowest card to each of the others.

The tubes are simulated cables. They sag or float with gravity, rest on the table and swing when a card moves.
