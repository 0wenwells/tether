One card grows a tube. Two cards tether it.

  1. Print the card sheet at 100%. The black squares must be 50 mm.
  2. Show one card for a tube with a free end, or two cards (15–40 cm apart) to pin both ends.
  3. Cards can be printed, or shown on a phone screen.

The tube is a simulated cable. With two cards it is pinned to both. It sags, rests on the table and swings when you move a card. Its cross-section morphs from one card's shape to the other's.
